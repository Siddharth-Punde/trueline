# Example Findings — Demo Narration Notes

These three sample findings illustrate the kind of flags TrueLine produces. They describe a fictional brand and impersonator pair so narrators can use them on camera without naming any real business. All findings are framed as suspected or unverified — none are confirmed as fraud or a scam.

---

## Finding 1 — Unofficial phone number (maps listing)

**Brand:** FreshCart (fictional) · **Impersonator:** "Fresh Cart Customer Care" (unverified listing)

A Google Maps listing surfaced by a "FreshCart customer care" search displays a phone number that does not match any of FreshCart's officially published numbers. The listing also appears as an unclaimed listing on Maps — meaning nobody has verified it as the genuine brand page. A customer calling that number could be connecting to an unrelated operator.

---

## Finding 2 — Ad pointing off the official domain

**Brand:** PayNest (fictional) · **Impersonator:** "paynest-support.com"

A paid Google ad for a PayNest refund query links to `paynest-support.com` rather than PayNest's official domain. The ad sits in a top position on the search results page, so a customer looking for help would see it first and might enter personal details on a look-alike site that is not the real brand's property.

---

## Finding 3 — Advertiser mismatch on Ads Transparency

**Brand:** SwiftMed (fictional) · **Impersonator:** "MedCare Plus" (unverified advertiser)

An Ads Transparency lookup on SwiftMed's official domain returns an advertiser name that matches neither SwiftMed nor any of its known aliases. The advertiser appearing on the domain is an unrelated entity, suggesting the ad account running under the brand's domain may not be genuinely controlled by the brand.

---

> **Note for demo narration:** Every finding above is backed by stored SERP evidence items (each carrying an `evidence_id`). TrueLine never invents a finding without a source result — the linked evidence snippet in the report lets viewers trace each flag back to the exact search result that produced it.
